# VibeCheck × Apne Aap Women Worldwide: partnership brief

*For Ruchira Gupta and the Apne Aap team. Follow-up to Carolina's email.*

## What VibeCheck is

A free, open-source tool that gives someone a second opinion before they trust a job offer, a message, a date or a "work abroad" opportunity. It reads the whole situation for the warning signs that precede trafficking and scams (upfront fees, documents held, travel arranged by a stranger, secrecy, urgency, pressure to move to a private chat), checks the organisation against official registers, and ends with what to consider and where to get confidential help. It never issues a verdict on a person; it reports indicators and links every fact to its source.

Live at https://carolina-moron.github.io/vibe-check. Built at the Microsoft Global Hackathon (Hack for Good), New York, by Carolina Pernambuco Moron (NYU), Dr. Obianuju Okafor and Elena Kennedy (Microsoft).

## Why Apne Aap

Apne Aap works where recruitment happens: with women and girls in the communities traffickers target first. VibeCheck works on the other side of the same moment, the message or offer that starts it. Together they close a gap: Apne Aap knows what a warning looks like from inside a community; VibeCheck turns that knowledge into a check anyone can run on a phone, in their own language, and into reports a reviewer can send to the platform or company involved.

## What we are asking

1. **Be the nonprofit partner** for the Hack for Good submission (name and logo on the site and the project page; no cost, no obligation beyond a conversation).
2. **One hour with organisers or survivors' advocates** to review our warning signs against how recruitment actually happens in the communities Apne Aap serves: what we miss, what we get wrong, what words people use. We will add Hindi and Bengali phrasing to the detection rules from that session.
3. **A pilot**: Apne Aap staff or community leaders try the check with people who bring them an offer, and tell us what happened. We report back the numbers (checks run, warning signs found, reports acted on) on the site.

## What Apne Aap gets

- A tool its organisers can use today, free, with no data collected about the people they help. Nothing is stored unless someone chooses to submit an anonymous report.
- Hindi and Bengali versions of the check, prioritised because of this partnership (Azure AI Translator, reviewed by Apne Aap).
- Survivor stories told the way Apne Aap wants them told: first-person scripts and AI-avatar videos that protect identities, with Apne Aap's review before anything is published.
- Reports to platforms and companies drafted by our agent and approved by a person, so cases Apne Aap sees can lead to postings being removed.
- Co-authorship on anything we publish from the pilot.

## Safeguards

- Checks run on organisations and situations, never background checks on private individuals.
- The agent drafts reports; a human approves each one. No automatic accusations.
- Personal details are removed on the device before anything is sent. No names, contacts or IP addresses are stored.
- Open source: Apne Aap can inspect, fork or host it.
