Job Risk Search

Check a job offer, company or recruiter for scam and human-trafficking red flags. Every flag shows its evidence and source.

Searches FBI Wanted and federal court dockets live, then links to public offender registries and maps. Results are possible name matches, not identity matches. Many people share a name. They never count toward a risk score. Confirm with photo, date of birth or location before drawing any conclusion.

If you or someone you know may be a victim of trafficking: US National Human Trafficking Hotline 1-888-373-7888 (text 233733). Report job scams to the FTC or FBI IC3.

Automated indicators, not legal findings. The reports dataset is demo data during development.
