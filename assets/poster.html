The problem

Trafficking and scams start the same way: an offer that looks easy, a friendly message, a little urgency. By the time someone realises they're trapped, they've lost money, documents or the people around them.

Upfront feesPassport takenJob with no addressCamera "broken"Crypto opportunityDeepfake bossCloned voiceKeep it secret

The solution

Vibe Check reads a whole situation, not just a link. It combines warning-sign rules from ILO, FTC and FBI guidance with official registers, enforcement records and country risk context, then explains what to consider and where to get help.

What's happening

Why it stands out in Hack for Good

  • Prevents harm before it happens, for anyone with a phone.
  • Every fact links to its source. No check ever returns "clear".
  • Survivor stories and first-person retellings build empathy.
  • Open source, no build step, nothing stored without consent.
  • Ready for NGOs, campuses and platforms to embed.
