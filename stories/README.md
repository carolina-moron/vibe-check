# First-person stories

Each story here retells a real case from `data/cases/` in the first person. They show what the warning signs look like from the inside, and how each case came to light.

**These are dramatised retellings, not testimony.** The narrators are composites. They are not real victims, and nothing here is a quote from any person. The facts in each story (fees, places, dates, charges, outcomes) come from the public sources listed at the end of it. The feelings and small details are written to make the pattern easy to recognise. Where a case is still only a charge, the story says so.
