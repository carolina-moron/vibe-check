# A student visa and a house to clean

*Dramatised retelling of the Bolaji Bolarinwa case, New Jersey, 2015–2016. The narrator is a composite, not either of the real women in the case.*

She promised me a chance to come to America. I came on a student visa, thinking I would study and help in her home a little.

Once I arrived in Moorestown, she took my passport. I cleaned and cared for her children from morning until night, for almost no pay. She watched me all the time. I wasn't allowed to go out or talk to people. When I didn't obey, she shouted and hit me, and she threatened to hurt my daughter.

**How it came out.** The case reached federal prosecutors. After a two-week trial, a jury found Bolarinwa guilty of two counts of forced labour, two counts of document servitude and one count of harbouring a person for financial gain. In May 2025 she was sentenced to 45 months in prison, a $35,000 fine and $87,518.72 in restitution to the women.

## Warning signs
- A host or employer holds your passport.
- You work around the clock for little or no pay.
- You aren't allowed to leave, go online or talk to others.
- Threats against you or your family.
