# A hundred euros a day in Athens

“They found me near Parque Lleras. A job as a waitress in Athens, a hundred euros a day, a room and meals included. In Medellín that is a fortune, and they made it sound ordinary.”

“In Greece they took my passport and my things. Then they told me what I owed: for the flight, the room, the papers. Between one and three thousand euros. I would pay it back, they said, and not by waiting tables. I was moved between Athens and Thessaloniki. There was violence, and I could not call home.”

**How it came out.** Three members of the network were arrested in December 2024. In December 2025, under plea agreements with Colombia's Fiscalía General, a court in Medellín sentenced two of them to six years and seven months and one to three years and two months for trafficking.

## Warning signs
- Pay far above the going rate for simple work, with housing and food thrown in.
- Someone takes your passport on arrival.
- A debt appears that you never agreed to.
- You are cut off from your family.
