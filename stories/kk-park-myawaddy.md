# The job in Bangkok

“The offer came in an online message: good pay, a job in Thailand, flights arranged. I checked nothing, because there was nothing to check. There was just a person in a chat.”

“In Bangkok someone met me and drove me north. Near Mae Sot we crossed the Moei river, and I understood too late that we were in Myanmar. The place was a walled compound. Guards took my phone and passport. My job, they said, was to message strangers online and talk them into fake investments. If I refused or missed my targets, there would be violence.”

**How it came out.** Pressure built from outside. The UN reported that hundreds of thousands of people had been trafficked into online scam work across Southeast Asia. In May 2025 the US Treasury sanctioned the Karen National Army and its leader, Saw Chit Thu, saying survivors of KK Park had seen guards wearing KNA insignia. In September 2025 it sanctioned She Zhijiang and the Yatai companies behind Shwe Kokko. In October 2025 Myanmar's military raided KK Park and arrested 2,198 people, and more than 1,500 people from 28 countries fled across the border into Mae Sot.

## Warning signs
- A job offered only through chat, with no employer you can verify.
- Travel arranged for you to a border region.
- Someone takes your phone or passport "for safekeeping."
