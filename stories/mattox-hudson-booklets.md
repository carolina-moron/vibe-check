# $5,000 a week folding booklets

“The ad said I could work from home assembling booklets: $20 a booklet, up to $5,000 a week. I needed something I could do around my kids.”

“There was an initiation fee. People paid anywhere from $50 to $500, usually by money order. I sent mine and waited for the materials.”

“Most people never got any. The ones who did, and did the work, were never paid. When I searched later, I found the same offer under other company names: Atlanta's Mailing Company, Central Mailing Company, Brown Staffing Services and more. There were at least 14.”

**How it came out.** More than 200 people paid. In August 2012 a federal grand jury in the Northern District of Georgia indicted Detrick Mattox and Jasmine Hudson of Ellenwood, Georgia, on conspiracy and mail fraud charges. An indictment is an allegation, not a conviction.

## Warning signs
- Pay that is far too high for simple work.
- You pay to start the job.
- Payment by money order, which is hard to trace or reverse.
- The same offer under many business names.
